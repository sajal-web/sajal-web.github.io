Privacy Policy for Compress PDF : PDF Tools

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Thank you for using Compress PDF : PDF Tools ("we", "our", or "the app"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Android application.

1. 100% On‑Device Document Processing (Files We Do Not Collect)

Your documents and files remain strictly private. We do not collect, upload, store, or transmit any of your PDF files, photos, scanned ID cards, extracted text, or digital signatures to our servers. All document processing—including PDF and image compression, merging, splitting, PDF-to-image, image-to-PDF, OCR text extraction, PDF editing, ID card print layout, and digital signature verification—happens 100% locally on your device.

2. Permissions We Use and Why

  • Storage Access (All Files Access / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE & Storage Access Framework): Requested optionally so the "All PDFs" document manager can locate, list, view, and organize PDF files stored across your device folders, and save your processed files locally. Your files never leave your device.
  • Camera & Document Scanner (Google ML Kit): Used only when you actively scan physical documents or ID cards into PDF format on your device.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to load advertisements via Google AdMob, sync anonymous app usage analytics and remote configuration via Google Firebase, check for Google Play app updates, and perform online certificate revocation checks (OCSP/CRL) when verifying PDF digital signatures.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for serving relevant ads, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID anytime in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Third‑Party Services & Data Collection

While we do not collect your personal files, the app integrates standard Google services that automatically collect certain device and usage information:

  • Google AdMob & Mediation Partners: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements (banners, interstitial, and app open ads). AdMob and its certified mediation partners may collect and use your device's Advertising ID, coarse IP-based location, device identifiers, and ad interaction metrics (impressions and clicks) to provide, personalize (where consented), and measure ads.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Remote Config): We use Firebase Analytics to understand aggregated, anonymous app usage patterns (such as screen views, feature engagement, and session duration) and Firebase Remote Config to dynamically tune app and ad settings without requiring an app update.
  • Google Play Services (ML Kit, In‑App Updates & Reviews): Used for on-device document scanning/OCR and official Google Play update and review prompts.

You can learn more about how Google collects and processes data at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Data Security & Retention

All processed PDFs, thumbnails, and history items are stored locally in your device's internal storage or folders you explicitly choose. You can permanently clear your local app cache and history at any time inside the app via Settings → Clear Cache & History or by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is a general-audience productivity utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page and inside the app.
